About BlockWise

A more personal approach to property care

BlockWise Cyprus was created for property owners, residential buildings and yacht owners who want reliable, transparent and responsive local support in Limassol.

Why BlockWise exists

Looking after property often means phone calls, contractors, bills, common expenses, repairs and constant follow-up. For overseas owners and volunteer Management Committees, this can quickly become a burden. BlockWise brings that responsibility together through one organised local point of contact.

Local to Limassol

We are starting in Limassol so we can provide personal presence and practical access to the properties and buildings we serve.

Greek & English

Service and communication in both Greek and English, particularly important for property owners living outside Cyprus.

Personal Accountability

Our service is built around personal contact, clear responsibility and consistent follow-up.
