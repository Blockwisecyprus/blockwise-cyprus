Property Care

Local care when you are not here

BlockWise acts as your trusted local point of contact in Limassol, helping ensure your property is checked, maintained and ready when you need it.

Ideal for

Overseas property owners, holiday-home owners, frequent travellers, investors, vacant properties and seasonally used apartments, houses and villas.

Regular Property Inspections

Checks can include doors and windows, signs of leaks, humidity, utilities, general condition, security concerns and external areas, with photo updates where agreed.

  • Doors, windows and general security
  • Visible water leakage or humidity
  • Electricity and water supply
  • Air-conditioning visual condition
  • Pool and garden condition where applicable
  • Photo updates after agreed inspections

Key Holding

Secure keyholding and authorised access for contractors, maintenance, emergencies, cleaning, deliveries, inspections and owner arrival preparation.

Maintenance Coordination

We help identify the issue, contact suitable contractors, obtain quotations where appropriate, arrange access, coordinate the work and follow up completion.

External contractor costs are approved and charged separately.

Before You Arrive

We can coordinate cleaning, electricity and water checks, air-conditioning, pool preparation, gardening, minor repairs and other agreed requirements before you return to Cyprus.

Emergency Support

When you are outside Cyprus, you can have a local point of contact for water leaks, electrical issues, access problems, security concerns or property damage.

Every property is different. Send us your details and we will contact you to understand what you actually need.
